Moonshine Ghost Viewer v2

Your Super Mario Sunshine disc

v2 draws the game's own stages, straight from your copy of the game, with the game's own model and material code. Pick your disc image (.iso or .gcm, any region) or a folder you extracted it to with Dolphin. It's read here in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Dolphin's compressed images (RVZ, WIA, NKit) need converting to ISO first: right-click the game in Dolphin, Convert File, ISO.

The first viewer still works without a disc.
